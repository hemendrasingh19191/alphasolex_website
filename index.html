Government Support & Customer Value

Schemes that improve your solar economics.

Indicative scheme information — eligibility, sanction, benchmark cost and portal/DISCOM rules apply.

Scheme / Support Who Can Benefit Indicative Support Important Note
PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana Eligible residential rooftop consumers ₹30,000/kW for first 2 kW; ₹18,000/kW for the 3rd kW; maximum ₹78,000 for systems above 3 kW. Residential grid-connected rooftop systems; subsidy is subject to current portal rules and DISCOM verification. Commercial/industrial consumers do not receive this residential CFA.
GHS / RWA common facilities Eligible group housing societies / RWAs Up to ₹18,000/kW for eligible common facilities, including EV charging, within scheme limits. Eligibility and capacity limits apply; confirm through the applicable national/state portal.
CREDA – Chhattisgarh state support Eligible categories under CREDA programmes CREDA publishes category-wise state grants. Examples include off-grid solar and solar irrigation support. Rates and eligibility can change by programme/category. Final benefit should be checked with CREDA/CSPDCL before financial commitment.
CREDA Solar Irrigation Pump Eligible farmers / community / government organisations Published CREDA schedule: up to ₹50/W or ₹2 lakh per plant for eligible pumps up to 5 HP. Applicable criteria, approved models and programme availability must be verified with CREDA.
PM-KUSUM Eligible agricultural beneficiaries / project entities For eligible pump components, central support can be 30% (or 50% in specified special-category regions) of benchmark/tender cost, with state contribution rules. Ground-mounted Component-A historically covered 500 kW–2 MW projects. Current availability/extension must be confirmed with the state implementing agency.

Residential grid-connected rooftop systems; subsidy is subject to current portal rules and DISCOM verification. Commercial/industrial consumers do not receive this residential CFA.

GHS / RWA common facilities

Who: Eligible group housing societies / RWAs

Support: Up to ₹18,000/kW for eligible common facilities, including EV charging, within scheme limits.

Eligibility and capacity limits apply; confirm through the applicable national/state portal.

CREDA – Chhattisgarh state support

Who: Eligible categories under CREDA programmes

Support: CREDA publishes category-wise state grants. Examples include off-grid solar and solar irrigation support.

Rates and eligibility can change by programme/category. Final benefit should be checked with CREDA/CSPDCL before financial commitment.

CREDA Solar Irrigation Pump

Who: Eligible farmers / community / government organisations

Support: Published CREDA schedule: up to ₹50/W or ₹2 lakh per plant for eligible pumps up to 5 HP.

Applicable criteria, approved models and programme availability must be verified with CREDA.

PM-KUSUM

Who: Eligible agricultural beneficiaries / project entities

Support: For eligible pump components, central support can be 30% (or 50% in specified special-category regions) of benchmark/tender cost, with state contribution rules.

Ground-mounted Component-A historically covered 500 kW–2 MW projects. Current availability/extension must be confirmed with the state implementing agency.
